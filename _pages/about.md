I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering](https://www.eee.hku.hk/) at The University of Hong Kong, working with [Prof. Kaibin Huang](https://huangkaibin.github.io/).

My research explores how artificial intelligence can reshape the way wireless systems communicate, with a focus on **generative semantic communications**, **token communications**, **collaborative edge learning and inference**, and **massive IoT connectivity**. I am broadly interested in building intelligent, efficient, and scalable communication paradigms for the next-generation networks. If any of these topics resonate with you, feel free to reach out.

Prior to HKU, I received my Ph.D. from the Beijing Institute of Technology and the University of Surrey, advised by [Prof. Jun Zhang](https://ac.bit.edu.cn/szdw/jsml/txjsyjs/zhangj/index.htm) and [Prof. Zhen Gao](https://gaozhen16.eu.org/). I also had the privilege of working with [Dr. Mahdi Boloursaz Mashhadi](https://sites.google.com/view/mahdimashhadi), [Prof. Pei Xiao](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/pei-xiao), and [Prof. Rahim Tafazolli](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/rahim-tafazolli) at Surrey, and [Prof. Deniz Gündüz](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/d.gunduz) at Imperial College London.
