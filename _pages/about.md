I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.ece.hku.hk/) at The University of Hong Kong, working with [Prof. Kaibin Huang](https://ece.hku.hk/people/huangkb/) (IEEE Fellow, Head of Department).

My research explores how artificial intelligence can reshape the way wireless systems communicate, with a focus on **generative semantic communications**, **token communications**, **collaborative edge learning and inference**, and **massive IoT connectivity**. I am broadly interested in building intelligent, efficient, and scalable communication paradigms for the next-generation networks. If any of these topics resonate with you, feel free to reach out.

Prior to HKU, I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from the Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by [Prof. Jun Zhang](https://sis.bit.edu.cn/szdw/gccrc/zlkxj/33b0eb333872441f9e9bcf47a38a0618.htm) and [Prof. Zhen Gao](https://gaozhen16.github.io/), and a second Ph.D. from the University of Surrey, working closely with [Dr. Mahdi Boloursaz Mashhadi](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/mahdi-boloursaz-mashhadi), [Prof. Pei Xiao](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/pei-xiao), and [Prof. Rahim Tafazolli](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/rahim-tafazolli) (IEEE Fellow). I also spent time as a visiting researcher with [Prof. Deniz Gündüz](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/d.gunduz) (IEEE Fellow) at Imperial College London.
